PARCEL PARTITION

Partition parcel

Pick a method and enter its values. The drawing and results update as you change them.

Click once to start the line, move the cursor, then click again to finish. The line can start and end anywhere; every span where it runs through the parcel becomes a cut, so one arm of a concave parcel is enough. Press Escape to cancel.

Free: cuts follow the frontage course and cut angle, or use Auto fit and square splitting. Vertical: cuts run north–south, so parts sit side by side. Horizontal: cuts run east–west, so parts stack. Between two sides: pick one side for each end of the cut; the cut slides between them until every part has an equal share. Preview, then Accept.
Options and limits
Tries every frontage course and cut angle and keeps the split with the most square parts. Ties go to the frontage that shares the frontage most evenly. Part areas stay exact.

TOOLS

Convert GNSS files

RINEX is the receiver-independent exchange format. Check a receiver file, convert RINEX observations to a compatible format, or send them to the inspector. Closed proprietary formats need the receiver maker’s free RINEX export tool; Northstar never guesses at binary data.

Files stay on this computer. Validation and conversion happen locally; nothing is uploaded.

RINEX observations

Open one or more observation files to inspect their headers, session details and marker positions. Each file is read locally; nothing is uploaded.

Approximate positions only. A RINEX observation file stores one marker position in its header, not a coordinate for every epoch.
